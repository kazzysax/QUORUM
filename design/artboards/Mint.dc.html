After the mint

  1. 1Mint closesWhen the last stage ends or it sells out.
  2. 2Holders vote to swapPer asset group, with the Rhea route and a minimum output.
  3. 3Vaults are creditedAnyone can credit vaults, three NFTs per transaction.
  4. 4FallbackIf nobody swaps in time, plain NEAR is credited instead.

Everyone pays the same NEAR, but each NFT ends up holding its assigned asset, so values move apart after settlement. Contracts are not audited.
