An NFT that holds value
and has a say.

Quorum turns each NFT into an account of its own. What it holds can always be redeemed, and every holder shares control of the collection treasury. The rules live in the contracts, not with a team.

A vault in every NFT

Each NFT owns a keyless NEAR account. Its owner can add assets to it. Nothing leaves unless the NFT is burned.

One NFT, one vote

Holders govern the treasury. Spending, distributions and swaps happen only through passed proposals, after a timelock.

Liquid by design

Burn any NFT at any time to redeem 90% of what its vault holds. The other 10% returns to the treasury.

Assets decided by holders

You mint in NEAR. Once the mint closes, holders vote to swap each NFT's share into its assigned asset through Rhea.

Where your mint goes

  1. 01You pay in NEARThe stage price, plus a small storage cost that opens your NFT's vault account.
  2. 02Half goes to the DAOStraight into the collection treasury, which only holder votes can move.
  3. 03Half backs your NFTHeld for your NFT, then swapped into its asset and credited to its vault.

No admin keys. Just the rules.

Vaults have no keys and the treasury has no admin. What you see in the contracts is what happens.
